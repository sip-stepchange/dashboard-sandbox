No advertising, CRM or social platform exposes your own subscription or contract price through its reporting API, so these figures cannot be pulled automatically. Anything left blank shows as “Not entered” on the dashboard rather than as zero.
LinkedIn restricts Sales Navigator data to approved Sales Navigator Application Platform (SNAP) partners, so seat usage, InMail volume and SN-sourced leads cannot be read through the API. Seats and cost are recorded here so the dashboard can show them alongside the metrics it genuinely cannot retrieve.
